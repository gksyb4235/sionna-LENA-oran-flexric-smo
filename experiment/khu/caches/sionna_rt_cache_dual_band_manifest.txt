Runtime bandwidth note
----------------------
The cache contains frequency-, geometry-, array-, and RET-dependent channel paths.
Changing the ns-3 carrier bandwidth (for example, 15 MHz to 10 MHz at 3.5 GHz)
does not by itself require rebuilding this cache; ns-3 applies the runtime spectrum
and resource-block grid separately.
